# Code of Conduct

AI Basics VN adopts the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1.

In short: be respectful, be constructive, assume good faith, and keep
discussion focused on the work. Harassment, personal attacks, and publishing
others' private information are not tolerated in any project space.

This is a beginner-friendly project: there are no "stupid questions".
Be patient with learners, and explain rather than dismiss.

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or ban contributors who
violate these standards. Reports can be made privately through the channels
in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and will be handled confidentially.
